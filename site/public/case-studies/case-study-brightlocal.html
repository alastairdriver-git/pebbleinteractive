Design and prototyping for a platform rebuild

Pebble scoped and built working concepts for BrightLocal's new platform, tested them, and gave the product team a written recommendation and a prioritised list of improvements.

Client
BrightLocal
Sector
B2B SaaS
Period
June 2026 – ongoing
Role
Embedded in the Product Design team
The brief

BrightLocal keeps businesses' listings, rankings and reviews in step across Google, Apple Maps, Bing, Facebook, Yelp and data aggregators, and is rebuilding its platform. Product teams needed to test new directions before committing engineering time, working from requirement documents, an expert UX audit and BrightLocal's own design system.

What we did

Location Manager: discovery to recommendation

Ran a kickoff with the product team, checked the requirements document line by line and analysed the team's Jira board. Built five alternative layouts as working prototypes, with realistic data states. Ongoing research into usability and feasibility turned what the team learned into a prioritised list of improvements.

Bringing design and development together

Now embedded in BrightLocal's Product Design team, changing how design and development work together. Prototypes live in the design system repository and go through the same pull-request review as code. Pebble also writes usage guidance in the team's Storybook documentation and audits prototypes against the system to find what it is missing.

AI for design systems

Improving how product and design teams use AI, by making design system documentation work for people and AI agents alike. Earlier, scoped and built BrightLocal Anywhere under its own statement of work: ranking reports rendered inside AI assistants, with a mock data service and clear points where the real platform plugs in.

Outcome

The product team got what it needed to make decisions: a recommended direction with its trade-offs, the open decisions and assumptions to confirm, and a prioritised list of improvements, in a workflow the in-house team now shares.
