B2B SaaS · Local search

Design and prototyping for a platform rebuild

BrightLocal, a UK local SEO platform, is rebuilding its product. Pebble designed and tested concepts across the product, and now helps its design and development teams work as one.

Client
BrightLocal
Sector
B2B SaaS
Period
June 2026 – ongoing
Role
Embedded in the Product Design team
What we did

Product concepts

Working prototypes across the product, from listings and Location Manager to reviews, with ongoing research into usability and feasibility, ending in recommended directions and a prioritised list of improvements.

Design to code

Prototypes reviewed like code in the design system repository, with usage guidance written into Storybook.

AI for design systems

Improving how product and design teams use AI, by making design system documentation work for people and AI agents alike.

Outcome

Clear recommendations the product team could act on, and a way of working the in-house team now shares.
