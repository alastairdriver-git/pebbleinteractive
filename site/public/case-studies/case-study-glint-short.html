Design and prototyping for UK and US business accounts

Glint, a gold and silver payments platform with its own Mastercard, is launching business accounts. Pebble designed and prototyped them, and brought its design system into code.

Client
Glint Pay
Sector
Fintech
Period
August – November 2026
Role
Product design and prototype consultant
What we did

Business portal

Onboarding through buying, storing and selling gold and silver, priced with real market data, so Glint could show partners a credible business account before building it.

Regulated onboarding

A step-by-step flow built from the compliance spec, branching on business type to meet US and UK ownership rules.

Design system in code

Glint's app design system brought into code from Figma, alongside its brand guidelines.

Outcome

A working product to show partners, and a design system and specifications Glint's developers build from.
