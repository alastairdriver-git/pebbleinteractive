## When you contact us

If you use our [contact form](/contact) or email us, we keep what you send: your name, email address, and anything else you choose to tell us, such as your role, company, website, company size, what you need, budget and timing.

- **Why:** to reply to you and, if you want to work with us, to prepare a proposal. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests, or steps you've asked us to take before a contract.
- **Where:** in a database hosted by Supabase in the UK (London), and in our email. We use Resend to send us a notification when a form arrives.
- **How long:** if we don't end up working together, we delete your enquiry within 24 months. If we do, we keep it with the project records.
- **What we don't do:** we don't add you to a mailing list or share your details with anyone else for marketing.

## Buying a product

Paid and free downloads are sold through [Polar](https://polar.sh), which acts as the merchant of record. Polar collects your payment and billing details under its own privacy policy, and shares your name, email and order with us so we can support you. We never see your card details.

## Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify individual visitors.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Email [hello@pebbleinteractive.com](mailto:hello@pebbleinteractive.com) and we'll reply within a month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).
