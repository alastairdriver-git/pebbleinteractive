brief: Pocket Films needed an experience that could serve two very different audiences. Production teams sourcing locations, crew and equipment, and service providers looking to be discovered. The challenge was making a complex marketplace feel effortless, across a mobile app, a provider web platform and a system admin.

workstreams:
  - title: One shared design system
    body: A single token foundation every surface inherited from, so colour, type, spacing and components were defined once and cascaded to the iOS app, the provider platform and the admin, with light, dark and brand themes.
  - title: AI-powered discovery and the app
    body: Workflows for project creation, provider discovery, shortlisting and team collaboration, and the interface for Juno AI, which matches providers to live production needs rather than keywords.
