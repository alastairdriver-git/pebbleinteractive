## Designing and coding the same components

<p class="cs-sub">Owning the tokens end to end, from Figma through to the front end.</p>

As the bridge between design and engineering, we built the layout and component code ourselves in TypeScript and Tailwind CSS. Designing and implementing the same system meant nothing was lost in translation: what shipped matched the design exactly, stayed accessible, and stayed maintainable as the clinical interface grew.

<p class="cs-tag">Overview</p>

## Clinical interface design

<p class="cs-sub">An interface that works on the web and embedded in healthcare systems.</p>

Working with the clinical lead, a practising GP, we ran usability tests on wireframes to make sure workflows were feasible, easy and traceable.

Access to the right patient data was key. At all times the user, whether a nurse, pharmacist, doctor or specialist, knows exactly where they are in the patient record. Some users have access to one clinic (in the UK, a GP surgery) and others to a whole group of clinics. Their rights and permissions differ, but the experience is just as streamlined for each.

- **User experience:** intuitive workflows and interfaces that simplify complex medical data for diverse user groups.
- **User interface:** a cohesive design system that adapts to different healthcare settings.
- **Branding:** a platform that reflects Metadvice's mission and values, with a consistent experience throughout.
- **Front-end development:** layout and component code in TypeScript and Tailwind CSS, responsive, accessible and maintainable.

## Real outcomes from the Ball Tree pilot

<p class="cs-sub">436 patients reviewed across primary and secondary prevention.</p>

A six-month pilot at the Ball Tree GP practice tracked 908 patients to see whether embedding decision support in the clinical workflow could measurably improve cardiovascular outcomes. The figures below are based on the 436 patients with three- or six-month follow-up data.

<div class="cs-metrics">
  <div><strong>35%</strong><span>Patients maintained or improved their cardiovascular risk score</span></div>
  <div><strong>3.8</strong><span>Predicted cardiovascular events avoided across the cohort</span></div>
  <div><strong>£35k</strong><span>Estimated savings to the healthcare system</span></div>
</div>
