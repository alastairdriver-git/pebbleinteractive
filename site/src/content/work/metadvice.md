workstreams:
  - title: Clinical interface design
    body: Workflows tested with the clinical lead, a practising GP, through usability sessions on wireframes, so every user always knows where they are in the patient record.
  - title: Decision support
    body: Dozens of risk factors, vitals and history surfaced where they matter, with the most relevant signals rising to the top based on patient risk.
    image: /work/metadvice/metadvice-gallery01-image1.jpg
    imageAlt: QRISK cardiovascular risk score

  - title: Design and code in step
    body: Layout and component code built in TypeScript and Tailwind CSS, so what shipped matched the design exactly and stayed accessible and maintainable as the interface grew.

outcome: A six-month pilot at the Ball Tree GP practice tracked 908 patients. Of the 436 with follow-up data, 35% maintained or improved their cardiovascular risk score.
