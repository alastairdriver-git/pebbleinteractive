## A design system that scales across products and countries

<p class="cs-sub">Laying the foundations for a design system that is well defined in Storybook and design tools.</p>

The idea of a design system was seeded, then implemented through an initiative called "Lift and Shift", which moved the existing designs onto the new system. It is now used in four countries and includes right-to-left language support.

<p class="cs-tag">Overview</p>

## Financial interface design

<p class="cs-sub">Designing the interface to work on the web, for providers and pension holders.</p>

Pebble helped deliver the Keystone design system and was integral to the redesign of every application that runs on the Smart platform, which spans B2C, B2B and internal administration. These applications are used by Smart Pension UK, Allied Irish Bank and Zurich Insurance Group.

- **User experience:** intuitive workflows and interfaces that simplify complex retirement data for diverse user groups.
- **User interface:** a cohesive design system that adapts to different brands.
- **Collaboration:** working with product managers, developers and stakeholders to align design with business objectives and user needs.
- **Innovation strategy:** helping define an innovation roadmap, beta programme and experimentation strategy.
- **Feature delivery:** more than 15 beta features delivered, five of them adopted into the Keystone platform.
- **Design system set-up:** the design system function established from scratch, defining the Figma design and React front-end processes.

<p class="cs-tag">Engagement</p>

## Increased user engagement by 24%

<p class="cs-sub">Using email, SMS and other channels to encourage active participation.</p>

Engagement with financial products, especially pensions, is a hard problem to crack. We designed campaigns that encouraged engagement through different hooks, and A/B tested every image and line of copy with small cohorts to optimise the results.
