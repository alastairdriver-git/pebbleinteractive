workstreams:
  - title: The Keystone design system
    body: Tokens, Figma libraries and a React front end built from scratch, then rolled out through a "Lift and Shift" programme that moved existing designs onto the new system. Now live in four countries, including right-to-left languages.
  - title: Platform redesign
    body: Every application on the Smart platform redesigned on Keystone, with an innovation roadmap and beta programme that delivered more than 15 beta features, five of them adopted into the platform.
  - title: Engagement campaigns
    body: Email, SMS and in-app campaigns to get savers engaging with their pensions, with every image and line of copy A/B tested on small cohorts.
