Applying to a supplier list usually means a due diligence form that asks for a dozen policies at once. Most small studios don't have them written down, even though they already work that way.

We wrote these for our own application to a public sector consultancy list. They're short, specific to a small company with no employees beyond its directors, and written in plain English rather than legal boilerplate. Adapt the details, have the directors adopt them, and you can answer the form honestly.

This pack is a starting point, not legal advice. Check anything that matters to your business with a professional.
